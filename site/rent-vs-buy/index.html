Rent vs buy: find your breakeven year

Two households, the same budget. The buyer pays the down payment and closing costs; the renter invests that cash. Every month, whoever pays less for housing invests the difference. Net worth is what each would walk away with if they sold that month, after selling costs and capital-gains tax.

Read before you rely on this

Educational model, not financial advice. It ignores the mortgage-interest deduction (which can shorten the breakeven, especially for single filers), PMI below 20% down, HOA fees, rent deposits, and moving costs, and assumes constant growth rates. Defaults: NAR median price (Oct 2026), Freddie Mac 30-yr rate (12 Nov 2026), Zillow single-family rent (Oct 2026), ATTOM property tax, Insurify insurance. Full sources are in the video description.
