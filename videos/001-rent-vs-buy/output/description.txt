We built a month-by-month model of renting vs buying with November 2026 data — same budget for both households, every assumption sourced — and found the breakeven year. KEY ASSUMPTIONS
• Home price $416,700 (NAR median, Oct 2026); 20% down; 30-yr fixed at 7.11% (Freddie Mac, 12 Nov 2026)
• Rent $2,243/mo (Zillow single-family ZORI, Oct 2026), growing 3%/yr
• Home appreciation 4%/yr; renter's portfolio 7%/yr; 15% capital-gains tax on the renter's gains
• Property tax 0.9%, insurance $3,012/yr (+3%/yr), maintenance 1%/yr, buyer closing costs 2%, selling costs 6%
• Each month the household with cheaper housing invests the difference. Mortgage-interest deduction not modelled (it can shorten the breakeven, especially for single filers).
• Net worth = what each household would walk away with if they sold/liquidated that month.
