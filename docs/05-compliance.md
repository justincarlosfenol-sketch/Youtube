# 05 — Policy & Compliance Playbook

This business must survive policy changes. The standard below is deliberately stricter than the minimum. *Re-check YouTube's Help Center before launch; policies change, and the summaries below come from reporting as of Nov 2026.*

## 1. Monetization eligibility and "inauthentic content"
YouTube's YPP policy (renamed from "repetitious" to "inauthentic content" in July 2025) excludes mass-produced, templated or repetitive content, judged **at channel level**.

**Our safeguards (every video):**
- [ ] **Original analysis:** a model unique to the video with its own computed results. No two videos share the same script skeleton *text*; they share a *framework*, not wording.
- [ ] **Human creative control:** a human edits the script and approves the final cut (`approval.json` is required by the upload tool).
- [ ] **Human voice preferred:** final narration recorded by a human (the operator or a contracted voice actor). The TTS voice is for drafts. If a synthetic voice is ever published, tick YouTube's *altered or synthetic content* disclosure **and** say so in the description.
- [ ] **No stock-footage montage padding.** Visuals are generated from our data, or are licensed assets that we materially transform.
- [ ] **Cadence:** 1 flagship per week. Never bulk-upload.

## 2. Copyright and asset rights
| Asset | Source | Rights |
|---|---|---|
| Charts, motion graphics, logo, thumbnails | Generated by our code | Owned |
| Fonts (Inter, Fraunces, JetBrains Mono) | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | Commercial use and embedding allowed; licence files in `brand/fonts/` |
| Music beds and sound effects | Procedurally synthesized by `breakeven/audio.py` | Owned (original). Can be swapped for YouTube Audio Library or a licensed library (Epidemic Sound / Artlist) later |
| Draft narration | Kokoro-82M TTS (Apache-2.0 model weights) | Commercial use allowed; a synthetic voice still needs disclosure if published |
| Data | Public statistics (Freddie Mac, NAR, Zillow research, BLS, Census, FRED) | Facts aren't copyrightable; we cite every source on screen and in the description. Don't reproduce proprietary datasets wholesale; use figures and derived results |
| Third-party logos and brands | Not used in thumbnails or graphics without a reason | Nominative mentions in narration only |

## 3. Financial content (YMYL) rules
- **Educational framing:** "here's how the math works and which inputs matter". Never "you should buy X". Every description carries the standard disclaimer (see `breakeven/metadata.py`).
- **No performance promises:** the QA linter blocks "guaranteed", "risk-free returns", "can't lose", "get rich quick" and similar.
- **Every factual number has a source ID** in the script (`[S3]`). QA fails if a script cites an ID that is missing from `sources.yaml`.
- **Assumptions on screen** whenever results are shown.
- **Corrections policy:** errors get a pinned comment within 24 h; material errors get an on-screen fix in a re-upload with a correction note.
- **Never** promote crypto schemes, trading signals, high-risk leverage products, or "investment opportunities" from sponsors.

## 4. Disclosures
- **Sponsorships:** tick "includes paid promotion" in YouTube Studio for every sponsored video, say "this video is sponsored by…" verbally at the start of the integration, and add a description line. (FTC Endorsement Guides.)
- **Affiliate links:** a description line *above* the links: "Some links below are affiliate links; if you use them we may earn a commission at no cost to you. We only include products relevant to the decision in this video." (FTC.) Many financial affiliate programs also have their own advertiser compliance rules (approved claims only), so check each program's terms before writing copy.
- **Synthetic media:** as described in §1.

## 5. Advertiser-friendliness
Personal finance is inherently advertiser-friendly. Avoid profanity in the first 30 seconds, graphic imagery, and sensationalized tragedy ("lost everything") framing in thumbnails.

## 6. Community guidelines
No misleading metadata (title/thumbnail must match content), no spam links, no comment-for-giveaway schemes, no sub-for-sub.

## 7. Business compliance (operator)
- Register the business (LLC recommended once revenue begins), get an EIN, and complete AdSense tax info (W-9 for US).
- Keep receipts for every expense in `finance/ledger.csv`; it feeds the dashboard.
- Affiliate programs require 1099 tax info.
- Keep API credentials out of git (see `.gitignore`; secrets live in `~/.config/breakeven/` or environment variables).
