# 02 — Business Strategy: Breakeven

## The business in one paragraph
**Breakeven** is a personal-finance media company that turns life's most expensive decisions into transparent, animated data stories. Each flagship video builds an original model from real public data, finds the **breakeven point** where one choice overtakes another, and shows the viewer which three or four inputs decide the answer for *them*. The YouTube channel is the top of the funnel. Around it we build owned assets: a free calculator website (one calculator per video), an email newsletter, paid spreadsheet models, and sponsorship/affiliate relationships with the financial products our viewers are already choosing between.

## Unique selling proposition
> **"We don't tell you what to do. We run the numbers so you can see it."**

- **Show the math, not the opinion.** Every number on screen comes from a stated model with cited inputs.
- **The breakeven moment** is our signature: two curves race and cross, the frame freezes, a marker drops, and the year/rate/age is called out. Every video has one.
- **"Your numbers" ending:** each video ends with the 3 inputs that change the answer, plus a free calculator, so viewers get a personal answer rather than a generic one.
- **Radical transparency:** sources are listed on screen and in the description; the model (spreadsheet/code) is downloadable; corrections are pinned.

## Target audience
**Primary: "The Decider"**, aged 25–44, US-based, household income $60k–$180k, facing a specific, large financial decision in the next 12 months (buying a first home, choosing a retirement account, buying a car, deciding on grad school). Analytical, distrustful of hype, wants to "understand it myself". Watches on desktop/TV during evenings and weekends; searches before deciding.

**Secondary: "The Optimizer"**, aged 30–55, already investing, FIRE-curious, enjoys data and simulations for their own sake. Drives browse traffic, comments and repeat viewing.

Why this audience is valuable: high purchasing power, in-market for financial products (high ad CPMs, $50–$400 affiliate payouts), and loyal to sources they trust.

## Format strategy (decided)

| Format | Role | Volume | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Long-form flagship (9–14 min)** | Revenue + watch hours + search equity | 1 per week | The 8,000-hour YPP bar (from Feb 2027) and $2–30 RPMs make long-form the business. Search topics compound for years. |
| **Shorts (35–55 s)** | Discovery funnel | 3 per flagship | Shorts RPM is cents; their job is to surface the most surprising number and link to the full video via YouTube's "related video" link. |
| **Annual refresh ("Rent vs Buy 2027")** | Re-rank evergreen winners with new data | 6–10 per year | Genuinely new analysis on proven topics, not reuploads. |
| **Community posts / polls** | Audience input into the next model | 1 per week | Engagement signal plus topic validation. |
| **Livestream "Ask the model"** (from month 4) | Loyalty, memberships | Monthly | Run viewer scenarios live through the calculator. |

**Not doing:** daily uploads, compilation videos, AI-generated stock B-roll montages, reaction content, or trending-news commentary without a model.

## Competitive moat (what compounds)
1. **Model library:** every video adds a reusable, verified model (and calculator). By video 50 we own the most comprehensive *visual* decision library in personal finance.
2. **Data pipeline:** automated refresh of rates, prices and returns makes annual updates cheap and fast.
3. **Owned visual language:** code-generated graphics in a consistent style that's hard to imitate with stock or AI footage.
4. **Trust and email list:** the assets that survive any algorithm change.

## Kill / pivot criteria (so we don't drift)
- After **12 flagship videos**: if the median video gets fewer than 300 views in 28 days **and** CTR is under 3%, the packaging is failing. Run a structured thumbnail/title overhaul (A/B with YouTube's "Test & Compare") before changing topics.
- After **25 videos**: if average view duration is under 35%, the format is failing. Shorten to 7–9 minutes and test a stronger on-camera presence.
- After **40 videos**: if under 2,000 subscribers, re-evaluate the niche using the analytics database (which topics *did* work), not gut feel.
